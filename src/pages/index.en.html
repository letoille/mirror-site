FAQ

Frequently asked

Will using Mirror get me banned?
Mirror never injects into or modifies the game process, memory or files. It only reads the game log, item text you copy yourself, and one screen frame for local OCR when you press the scan hotkey; pricing uses the official trade API — the same way the official trade site works. Always follow the game's Terms of Service.
Which realms and languages are supported?
Global, China and Taiwan realms, with an English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese interface. China-realm trade search needs a valid session cookie in settings.
What's the difference between the Mirror client and Mirror Web?
Mirror client is a Windows overlay application covering in-game operations: hover pricing, screen scanning, the campaign guide, regex presets, timers, currency rates, drop tallies, wealth tracking, cheat sheets, and writing loot filters and build plans into the game. Mirror Web (trade.kalandraeye.com) runs in the browser and contains four tools: a market that searches by modifier, a character page that evaluates gear via Path of Building, loot filter downloads and a regex generator. Both share one account.
Do I have to install the client to use Mirror Web?
Not always — it depends which tool. Loot filter downloads and regex generator work from the browser alone. Market search and character try-on require the client to be running: searches use your own official trade session (our servers issue no requests at all to the official trade API — a compliance requirement), and the Path of Building engine likewise runs inside the client process.
Does it support Path of Exile 1 (POE1)?
Yes. Mirror supports both Path of Exile 1 and 2, and most features (pricing, screen scan, campaign guide, regex presets, currency rates, timers, drop tallies, loot filters, wealth tracking) work in both. Wealth tracking is available in both; only the source of the assets differs — Path of Exile 1 reads your stash, Path of Exile 2 reads your public listings on the trade site. Either way you get tracked-asset value, profit over the period, and income per hour and per map. POE1 additionally values Thaumaturgic Dust and mercenary warrants.
Where does the installer come from?
From this site — download, run, done. All it needs is Windows 10 / 11.
Is Mirror free?
Yes. The download, item pricing, screen scanning, the campaign guide, regex presets, currency rates, timers, drop tallies, quick search, market prices and wealth tracking are all available as-is; several of them carry a quantity limit on the free tier. Membership unlocks Mirror Market (class-projected search, character try-on, the AI assistant), one-click loot-filter installs into the game and your GGG account, config cloud sync, faster market refresh, and higher limits on the features above; AI features are metered by usage. The loot filters themselves are not paid — anyone can download the files (sound packs included) from this site or the web app and drop them into the game folder by hand.
How do I use Mirror?
Download, install and run — the hotkeys are preconfigured. Hover an item in game and press the hotkey to price it; press Ctrl+D to scan and price a whole screen.
Does it work with a gamepad?
Yes. Pricing and every other feature can be triggered from either a keyboard shortcut or a gamepad button.
Do you upload my screenshots or data?
No. Item recognition runs entirely on your own PC; no screenshots or account details are uploaded, and pricing only calls the official trade API.
Why does the Tencent (CN) realm need administrator rights?
That client runs elevated. Unless Mirror is started at the same level, hotkeys and overlays have no effect over the game window.
Will it cover a fullscreen game?
No. Overlays are anchored to the game's client area — in fullscreen that is the whole monitor — and they hide themselves when you switch away.
