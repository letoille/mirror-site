关于 魔镜About Mirror

魔镜（Mirror）是一款免费的《流放之路》1 & 2（POE1 · POE2）桌面悬浮窗助手，帮你在不切出游戏的情况下查价、看攻略、统计收益。Mirror is a free desktop overlay assistant for Path of Exile 1 & 2 (POE1 · POE2) — price-check, follow guides and track profit without ever leaving the game.

01魔镜是什么What is Mirror

魔镜是一款运行在 Windows 上的《流放之路》桌面辅助工具，以「悬浮窗」的形式把常用信息叠加显示在游戏画面之上。你在游戏里悬停一件物品并按下热键，它会实时查询官方交易行的挂单价并显示在物品旁边——全程无需切出游戏、无需手动打开网页。除了查价，它还整合了截图识别、剧情引导、快捷正则、通货兑换、计时器、奖励掉落统计等一系列刷图常用功能。

Mirror is a Windows desktop companion for Path of Exile that overlays the information you need on top of the game. Hover an item in-game and press a hotkey — it looks up live listings from the official trade API and shows the price right next to the item, with no alt-tabbing and no browser needed. Beyond pricing, it bundles screen recognition, a campaign guide, regex presets, currency exchange rates, timers and drop tally into one tool.

02核心功能Core features

  • 物品查价：悬停物品一键查询官方交易行实时价格。Item pricing — one hotkey to query live official-trade prices.
  • 截图识别查价：一帧截图本地识别整屏物品名并批量查价。Screen scan — local OCR reads a whole screen of items and prices them.
  • 剧情引导：自动识别所在区域，跟随切换任务与导航。Campaign guide — detects your zone and follows along with tasks.
  • 快捷正则：把常用正则拖出来，一键复制到商店/仓库搜索框。Regex presets — one click to copy your regex into the search box.
  • 通货兑换 / 计时器：实时通货比率与全局+地图计时。Currency & timers — live rates and global + per-map timers.
  • 奖励计数 / 收益统计：统计刷图掉落与收益，POE1 还能做仓库估价与打金收益归因。Drop tally & wealth stats — track drops and profit; POE1 adds stash valuation and per-map attribution.

04安全与隐私承诺Safety & privacy

魔镜绝不注入、读取或修改游戏进程与内存，也不修改任何游戏文件。它只会：读取游戏的日志文件、读取你主动复制的物品文本、以及在你按下识别热键时截取一帧屏幕做本地 OCR。所有查价均通过官方交易行 API 完成，工作方式与官方交易网站一致。

物品识别全程在你本地电脑完成，不上传截图或账号信息。国服交易搜索需要你在设置中自行填入会话 Cookie，该信息仅保存在本地、仅用于向官方接口发起查询。请你同时自行遵守游戏的服务条款。

Mirror never injects into, reads, or modifies the game process or memory, and never modifies any game files. It only reads the game's log file, item text you copy yourself, and one screen frame for local OCR when you press the scan hotkey. All price lookups go through the official trade API, exactly like the official trade site.

Item recognition runs entirely on your own PC — no screenshots or account info are uploaded. The China-realm session cookie you enter is stored locally and used only to query the official endpoints. Please also follow the game's Terms of Service.
